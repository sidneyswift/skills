- infers the consulting lifecycle: leads → qualifying → discovery → proposal → negotiation →
  won/lost;

  into `knowledge/`;

- files the one-off pricing-calculator idea under `work/`, never as a top-level folder;
- marks inferred facts "draft — confirm".
