## Why this reference is useful

A relationship changes when a word takes a new role. The bowed line and hanging load make pressure visible; the second support creates a level composition. The final statement inherits that arrangement. Heavy type acts as the structure while lighter phrase text carries reading order. The narrow palette helps those two roles remain distinct.

This is more specific than “animate the text.” For an original piece, assign words or symbols meaningful structural jobs, then make the layout change express the relationship. Do not require this architectural metaphor for every typographic brief.

## Original build contract

1. **Assign roles.** Choose a reading phrase, a support, a load and an eventual resolution. Use the subject's own logic rather than copying this speech or palette. Keep a small identity map showing which element survives each phase.
2. **Define shared geometry.** Give the support a contact anchor. Compute the connecting rule from its endpoints and a controlled sag parameter. Attach suspension lines to positions on that same curve. This is authored geometry unless a solver is actually used.
3. **Separate reading from transformation.** Give each phrase a readable state before it rotates or becomes architecture. Maintain an unambiguous active reading line; a vertical support does not need to remain the main reading surface.
4. **Make alignment the consequence.** Let support arrival change the endpoint height or sag. Reaching a level, stable arrangement can carry the resolution without an extra flash or particle burst.
5. **Preserve evidence of the journey.** Retain enough identity and position history for the audience to connect earlier parts to the final composition. Inspect this with labels or simple boxes before fine typography.
6. **Prove ambitious transitions separately.** For a letter aperture, inspect before/inside/after views and show that the counter actually reveals the next scene. For a hidden reset, inspect occlusion coverage, reset timing and the loop seam. A prompt mentioning those effects is not proof they occurred.

Useful controls: time allowed for reading, structural type weight, endpoint height difference, sag, support arrival offset, spacing between text and load, and duration of the resolved hold. Tune these against the delivery size. Fixed pixel values and the source's 20-second format are not transferable requirements.

## Failure checks

- Trace one named element through reading, structural action and resolution. A sudden unrelated replacement weakens the causal story.
- Sample the support contact and cable endpoints before and after leveling. They should not detach during deformation.
- Read the current sentence at final display size without scrubbing. If reading requires pausing, simplify or extend the reading state.
- Test aperture and reset claims independently; do not inherit them from an overall resemblance to the reference.

Original adaptation prompt:

> Turn [short statement] into a typographic scene in which its words perform meaningful roles. Choose one relationship the audience can see changing. Preserve recognizable elements through reading, action and resolution; derive connected parts from shared anchors. Inspect the hardest transformation and final reading hold. Report which effects are observed and which remain intentions.
